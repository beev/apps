Record tests, then save, edit and share.

Simple to mark

One tap to add a fault, double tap to remove it. The total faults are counted automatically at the end of the test.

Edit after saving

Tests can be edited once saved. Just hit the Total Faults button to save again when you have finished tinkering.

Notes for your pupil

Add notes to a test and, if you like, send them to your pupil along with their completed DL25.

Send results by email

Email test results in one go. Automatically copy yourself in, and automatically include explanatory text with each email.

Sort your records

Sort saved tests by date or by candidate name, so any pupil's history is quick to find.

Statistics

Statistics across all your test records, filtered by mock test or actual test.
