A free written guide to learning to drive, covering everything from finding the
biting point to reading a spiral roundabout. It is the companion to the
[Lessons](/) app, so the sections here follow the same order your instructor
works through on the road.

Nothing here replaces time behind the wheel with a qualified instructor. What it
does is let you arrive at a lesson already knowing what a closed junction looks
like, or leave one able to go back over the observation sequence at your own
pace. Most learners find a briefing sinks in far better when they can read it
again the evening after driving it.

The diagrams are the same ones used in the app, drawn from the driver's point of
view wherever that is what matters.

## Where to start

If you have not driven before, begin with [Controls](@controls) and
[Position](@position). Those two sections cover everything that happens inside
the car and where the car should sit on the road, which between them account for
most of the first few lessons.

Once you are moving confidently, the remaining sections deal with the situations
you meet out on the road, roughly in the order most instructors introduce them.

## The sections

**[Controls](@controls)** — the cockpit drill, mirrors and blind spots, and every
pedal, lever and switch you need before the car moves at all.

**[Position](@position)** — where to place the car: moving off and stopping,
passing obstacles, following distances, lane discipline and bends.

**[Manoeuvres](@manoeuvres)** — the set-piece exercises, from parallel parking
and bay parking to reversing and the turn in the road.

**[Stopping](@stopping)** — following distance, planning ahead, what to do in a
skid, and the controlled stop you may be asked to demonstrate on test.

**[Pedestrian Crossings](@pedestrian-crossings)** — zebra, pelican, puffin and
the rest, and how to tell which one you are approaching.

**[Junctions](@junctions)** — give way and stop junctions: turning in and out of
them, and dealing with crossroads.

**[Traffic Lights](@traffic-lights)** — light sequences, filter arrows, box
junctions and turning right across traffic.

**[Roundabouts](@roundabouts)** — mini, small, medium, large and spiral, and why
the approach differs for each.

**[Dual Carriageways](@dual-carriageways)** — joining and leaving by slip road,
overtaking, and turning right where there is no slip road at all.
